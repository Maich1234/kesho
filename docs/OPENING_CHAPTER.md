# Chapter 01 — A Place Before Dark

## Dramatic question

Can you make room for tomorrow before the city starts deciding for you?

The protagonist is 19, has finished high school and arrives at Maweni district with C1,200 and a bag. C is fictional currency. This original district is an art and systems blockout, not a real place or an ethnic-region stand-in.

## Opening shot and first scene

An elevated view of a bus square, laundry above a courtyard, a delivery yard and a tea kiosk. The title introduces opportunity and obligation rather than a corruption tutorial. Create a character, select a family background, then learn who is waiting for you. The first playable challenge is to meet that contact and arrange a bed. There is no timer punishing the player while reading.

| Background | Opening connection | Accommodation |
|---|---|---|
| Family household | Aunt Sifa's neighbour, sent to meet you | Relative's spare room, C150 for three nights; household obligations recorded |
| Independent parent | Kito, an old school friend | Shared room, C240 for three nights |
| Community upbringing | Leila, a community coordinator | Hostel, C300 for three nights |

All backgrounds can use the community hostel. Selected relative personality is reserved for later deeper dialogue, not a hidden live modifier in v0.1.

## Places and cast

- **City square:** the opening contact. Calling home creates a relationship memory; asking about the fees office provides a clue, not certainty.
- **Courtyard:** Sifa manages a ledger, a household and conflicting demands. Listening while helping with supper reveals a documented fee waiver.
- **Dispatch yard:** Tendo provides limited documented work. A stranger offers a better-paying errand but avoids discussing its purpose.
- **Learning centre:** Rehema explains applications without promising admission. An outside broker sells access whose legitimacy is unclear until investigated.
- **Tea kiosk:** Nuru provides food, modest paid help and competing rumours. Humour comes from her personality and everyday frustration.

## Planning rules

Each day runs from 08:00 to 22:00; arrival day starts at 10:00. Conversations and activities declare their time/energy costs. Walking and reading are free. Three nights of accommodation are charged once and cover the entire slice. A meal restores energy once a day; sleep restores it to 90. One-off jobs and introductions cannot be farmed for infinite money. These limits are explicit prototype bounds, not the intended long-term labour simulation.

## Three playable directions

1. **Work:** sorting or documented deliveries create earned income and a future connection. Opportunity costs appear in time and fatigue.
2. **Education:** read the official notice, apply normally or discover the waiver through a relationship; investigate campus sponsorship without buying a promised admission.
3. **Shortcuts:** take the envelope errand or pay the broker. Some outcomes pay, some disappoint, and some create future obligations. The UI communicates clues rather than exact odds. The current slice records future hooks but does not yet play their later consequences.

All directions can overlap. This opening does not assign an irreversible class, morality score or destiny. Random outcomes are rolled once per committed decision and saved along with RNG state. Repeating a decision from an identical future checkpoint must not reroll it; changing the life seed or meaningful preceding decisions may change results.

## Closing scene

After the third night, show an individual summary based on actual actions: bed secured, money remaining, documented work, application leads and unresolved obligations. This is a chapter ending, not the character's final reckoning. The player can read and edit the private journal or begin another prototype life after explicit replacement confirmation.

## Acceptance criteria

A new player can understand how to find a bed, earn money and pursue an education lead without developer instructions. Every destination is reachable from every other destination. No legal choice creates a negative balance or exceeds the day's planning hours. Reloading preserves established decisions. Each background has an affordable route to accommodation and a legitimate education application. Private notes never appear in the summary payload.
