# Kesho research collection — 22 October 2026

## Scope and status

First public-source discovery collection for the proposed Kesho Life Director. This is a source catalogue and original design notes, not a downloaded corpus, a trained model, or an exhaustive Shujaaz archive. No gameplay code changed. Sources span selected historical and contemporary material relevant to 2007–2026; coverage of every year has not been established.

Access status: **P** = public page opened; **D** = discovered in search, underlying report not reviewed; **F** = retrieval attempted but failed. Opening an index or publication landing page does not mean its linked PDF was fully reviewed. All sources accessed/discovered 2026-10-22.

## How this feeds the proposed model

1. Research catalogue: source identity, URL, publication date, observation period, geography, methodology and retrieval status.
2. Evidence cards: short original factual summaries with exact page/table references and limitations. Each quantitative claim needs its denominator, unit, sample and uncertainty. No statistics extracted in this first catalogue.
3. Original authored scenarios: fictional people, institutions, dialogue and incidents; facts, allegations and beliefs remain distinct.
4. Approved runtime knowledge: only reviewed, appropriately licensed material and our original authored game facts. An index entry is never automatically a retrieval or training approval.
5. Evaluation examples: human-reviewed tests for continuity, causality, language, agency and representation. Hold out scenario families from training.

Suggested record fields: id, title, publisher, source_url, published_at, observation_start, observation_end, geography, population, methodology, access_status, original_summary, claim_status, limitations, topics, proposed_game_use, rights_status, training_approved, runtime_approved, reviewed_by, reviewed_at.

Default every external source to rights_status=unverified, training_approved=false, runtime_approved=false. Shujaaz website content additionally has restrictive terms documented in S17. Our original notes are research aids, not substitutes for reviewing source-specific rights. No copied comic panels, scripts, audio, interview transcripts, social-media comments, personal profiles or raw sensitive records were collected.

Never infer individual criminality from religion, ethnicity, gender or regional origin. Never equate a survey proportion with an NPC's probability of assault, death or conviction. Positive outcomes, friendship, humour, ethical success and cooperation need authored coverage alongside conflict. News reporting can identify candidate incidents, but allegations need status tracking and independent primary-source corroboration before adaptation.

## Gaps before an ML dataset can be called ready

- Complete Shujaaz comic/episode inventory and access to back issues are not established. Private research and raw interviews are not available here.
- Request a scoped licence if we want actual Shujaaz comics, images, audio, scripts, retrieval storage or model training. Ask separately about commercial mobile use, translation, adaptation, hosting, retention, attribution and participant-consent limits. Contact route: S18. No outreach authorised or sent.
- Review the underlying reports; retry failed official sources. Record exact methods and usable numerical tables only after review.
- Fill year-by-year gaps for 2007–2026; record publication and incident dates separately. This collection is Kenya-first, not representative of every African country.
- Add verified higher-education funding, housing, justice/prisons, disability inclusion, religion/community service, technology entrepreneurship and rural market sources.
- Build an original English/Kiswahili/Sheng dialogue set with consenting writers/performers and local editorial review. Do not scrape private chats or assume translated research text sounds natural.
- Review source and content rights before any retrieval index or training job. Keep personal respondent data out of the game.

The next deliverable is a reviewed evidence-card dataset and original scenario specifications. This catalogue does not approve implementation of the proposed Life Director or establish that any ML training has occurred.
