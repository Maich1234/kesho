# A Place Before Dark — Unity opening

This chapter is the college-entry transition at the beginning of the lifetime. Production must lead into campus life and then adult life; the fund-office cliffhanger is not the full game ending. The complete ordinary path targets 24 hours across all life stages, as specified in the [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

## Dramatic spine

The player arrives with a family connection, C1,200 and an interview. A small favour at the Opportunity Fund asks them to sign for money they have not received. The household makes the risk personal; campus advice keeps a legitimate route alive. The final contact is different for someone who signed, someone who gathered a reference and someone who stayed outside the deal.

The authored source is a short systems/blockout chapter. The approved 45–60-minute opening requires staged action, developed character performances, environmental investigation and playtesting. Do not stretch these dialogue lines into an hour with waiting.

These are design budgets. The current source does not implement all physical staging described in this table.

## What branches actually change now

- Paying accommodation vs helping changes cash and information about the fund.
- Documented delivery pays only once and can establish independent campus knowledge.
- Paying the fee produces a mismatched receipt; questioning it opens the envelope offer.
- Reading/recording the ledger establishes specific known facts. It is not proof of every alleged crime.
- Signing establishes exposure even if the envelope is returned; returning creates a useful record.
- Sharing with Rehema or Sifa establishes who was told. They do not learn private choices automatically.
- Accepted deal: bounded seeded variation between a visit and a phone demand.
- Copied reference without accepting: a potential witness comes forward.
- Ordinary route: a contact warns that someone asked about the player.
- Every ending retains an education or work plan. This is a chapter cliffhanger, not a lifetime ending.
